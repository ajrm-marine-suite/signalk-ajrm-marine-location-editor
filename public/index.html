Location catalogue

Backup and transfer

Export downloads the complete versioned catalogue. Merge retains unrelated locations. Replace Catalogue removes locations absent from the file. Both actions accept Location Editor catalogues and Harbour Editor v1 exports.

Messages
Ready.

Deleted locations

Deletions remain as versioned tombstones. Open history to restore an earlier snapshot as a new revision. Purge permanently removes every deleted location and its history.

Purging cannot be undone. Purged IDs remain blocked from automatic seeding and ordinary merges; replacing the whole catalogue can reintroduce them.
